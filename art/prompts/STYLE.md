# Библия стиля «Пепел между кадрами»

Этот блок вставляется В НАЧАЛО КАЖДОГО запроса к генератору (Gemini, Grok, GPT) — так
весь арт игры остаётся в одной манере. Текст для генератора — на английском (модели
понимают его точнее), пояснения для нас — по-русски.

---

```
STYLE BIBLE — "Ash Between Frames" (follow strictly, every time):

ART STYLE
- High-detail 32-bit era pixel art, like the best late PlayStation / Sega Saturn 2D games
  (Castlevania: Symphony of the Night, Vagrant Story backgrounds, modern "HD-2D"-like pixel art).
- Very fine, crisp, square pixels; small pixel size, rich detail; no blur, no soft airbrush,
  no painterly brush strokes, no anti-aliased vector look, no photo textures, no 3D render look.
- Hand-placed pixel clusters, deliberate dithering in gradients, 1-pixel highlights on edges.
- Dark melancholic gothic fantasy. Mood: a world that is forgetting itself.

CAMERA — ONE PROJECTION FOR THE WHOLE IMAGE
- Top-down 3/4 orthographic view, like a classic action-RPG map (Diablo-like but straight-on):
  seen from above at a steep angle, vertical walls slightly visible, NO perspective,
  NO vanishing point, NO converging lines, constant scale from top to bottom of the image.
- There is NO HORIZON in this view. The ground never recedes into the distance. The map ends at
  its top edge with a VERTICAL obstacle seen from the front: a city wall, a cliff, a building
  facade or a gate. Only a thin strip of sky may show ABOVE that wall's battlements.
- Never continue a road or ground "beyond" the top wall into a perspective landscape — that mixes
  two projections and makes the road look like it climbs up a vertical ladder.

LIGHT
- Night. Cold blue-black ambient light, soft moonlight from the TOP-LEFT.
- Warm amber lamplight pools (orange-gold) appear on the LEFT side = the living PAST.
- Crimson / magenta glow comes from erased areas and cracks on the RIGHT side = the ERASURE.
- Everything is wet after rain: stones have small cold-blue specular highlights,
  puddles are dark mirrors with thin light edges.

PALETTE (stay close to these colors)
- Night & stone: #05060C #0B0D14 #161925 #262B3A #343B4F #4A5470 #7B88A3
- Warm past (amber, autumn): #3A2010 #7A4A18 #B8721F #E0A040 #FFD27A #FFF4D0
- Erasure (crimson): #2A0610 #4A0C1E #86163A #C8284A #FF5A7A
- Nature (dark moss, olive grass): #1B1F1C #2E3A24 #3B4A2A #4F5430
- Rare magic accent (teal): #3AD0C0

MATERIALS
- Cobblestones: rounded irregular stones in rows, dark slate blue-grey, darker gaps,
  thin wet highlight on the top-left edge of each stone.
- Curbs: long granite blocks along the road edges.
- Stone balustrade: carved balusters (vase shapes) with a stone rail.
- Ground: dark brown-black earth, fallen orange/amber/rust autumn leaves, dark grass tufts.
- Erased ground: ash-grey dead soil, glowing crimson hairline cracks, missing square chunks
  of pixels like a corrupted video frame, scattered red pixels, broken stones.
- Void / chasm: pure black emptiness with torn crumbling edges lit by crimson cracks.

NEVER
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI, frames or borders.
- No characters, people, animals, monsters.
- No rain streaks or fog painted in (the game adds weather itself).
- Do not change the layout of the attached layout image.
```
